TelemetryBus: single-slot ForceStandByForAllRoles reader (capstone finding)

BotLogPatches.cs's `forced` field reads Plugin.ForceStandByForAllRoles.Value on
EVERY bot-spawn/stand-by-assigned event (not once per window), so unlike the
27 other config fields already folded into the window callback's cfg dump,
this one needs its own per-event resolution - same one-slot registered-reader
shape as TryAskBotStandBy/_botStandByPredicate above it, for the identical
reason (BotOwner-scoped stand-by system, exactly one producer).

TelemetryBus.RegisterForceStandByForAllRolesReader(Func<bool>) / internal
ForceStandByForAllRoles() added, fail-safe false if nothing registered.
BotLogPatches.cs's two forced-field writers switched from the now-broken
direct Plugin.ForceStandByForAllRoles.Value read (which resolved to Ranger's
OWN Plugin after the namespace switch, silently wrong assembly) to the bus
call.
